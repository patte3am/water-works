# Water Works

**Every Drop Makes a Difference**

Water Works is a quick, responsive browser game inspired by charity: water's
mission to bring clean and safe drinking water to communities around the world.
Collect clean water before time runs out, and avoid polluted water.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the live GitHub Pages site:

https://patte3am.github.io/water-works/

- Choose a difficulty and click **Start Game**:
  - **Easy:** 45 seconds, 15 points; slower spawns and fewer polluted drops.
  - **Normal:** 30 seconds, 20 points.
  - **Hard:** 20 seconds, 25 points; faster spawns and more polluted drops.
- Collect clean water for one point. Polluted water subtracts two points, but the
  score never drops below zero. Drops disappear when clicked or when their time
  on the board expires.
- Reach the goal to win, earn milestone messages at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%,
  and celebrate with confetti. Use **Reset Game** or **Play Again** to replay.
- Use the sound toggle to mute or enable the game's synthesized sound effects.
- Visit charity: water's website or donation page using the links on the game.

The project uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step or external
game engine is required. The game is keyboard accessible, responsive on desktop,
tablet, and mobile, and respects reduced-motion preferences. The charity: water
logo and branding assets are in `img/`.
